## 1. The Exact Addressability Requirement

Let the streaming learner receive a causal $D$-dimensional input stream $X_t \in \mathbb{R}^D$ at discrete time steps $t \in \mathbb{N}$.
To discover unknown delayed dependencies without prior knowledge of the true delay set $S^* = \{(i_1, k_1), \dots, (i_m, k_m)\} \subset \{0, \dots, D-1\} \times \{1, \dots, L_{\max}\}$, the learner must evaluate candidate correlations:
$$\hat{\rho}_{i, k}(t) = \mathbb{E}_t [e_t \cdot x_{i, t-k}]$$
This requires the history subsystem to satisfy the **Arbitrary Point Query Operator**:
$$\mathcal{Q}(i, k) \mapsto \hat{x}_{i, t-k}, \quad \forall i \in \{0, \dots, D-1\}, \quad \forall k \in \{1, \dots, L_{\max}\}$$

## 2. Worst-Case Information Storage Derivation

### 2.1 The Incompressibility of High-Entropy Streams
Consider an IID Gaussian streaming process:
$$x_{i, t} \stackrel{\text{iid}}{\sim} \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$$
Suppose each continuous sample is uniformly quantized into $B$ bits (giving $2^B$ discrete quantization levels across dynamic range $[-V_{\max}, V_{\max}]$).
At any time step $t$, the sliding temporal window contains:
$$N_{\text{samples}} = D \cdot L_{\max} \text{ independent random variables}$$

Because the sequence is independent and identically distributed:
1. **Mutual Information Between Time Steps is Zero:**
   $$I(x_{i, t}; x_{i, t-k}) = 0, \quad \forall k \ge 1$$
2. **Joint Shannon Entropy is Maximized:**
   $$H(X_{t-1}, \dots, X_{t-L_{\max}}) = \sum_{k=1}^{L_{\max}} \sum_{i=1}^D H(x_{i, t-k}) = D \cdot L_{\max} \cdot B \text{ bits}$$
3. **Kolmogorov Incompressibility Theorem:**  
   By Kolmogorov complexity theory, for any sequence generated by an independent, maximum-entropy process, the fraction of bit sequences of length $M = D \cdot L_{\max} \cdot B$ that can be compressed by more than $c$ bits is at most $2^{-c}$.
   Therefore, in the worst case (and with probability $1 - 2^{-c}$), **zero lossless compression is possible without discarding information**.

### 2.2 Fundamental Storage Theorem for Exact Addressability
> **Theorem (Lower Bound on Exact Point Addressability):**  
> If an input stream $X_t$ has independent time steps with entropy rate $h$ bits/sample, any history provider capable of reconstructing every sample $x_{i, t-k}$ with distortion $d(x, \hat{x}) \le \epsilon$ must maintain a persistent state of at least:
> $$\mathcal{S}_{\min} \ge D \cdot L_{\max} \cdot R(\epsilon) \text{ bits}$$
> where $R(\epsilon)$ is the rate-distortion function of the source. For zero distortion on $B$-bit quantized inputs:
> $$\mathcal{S}_{\min} = D \cdot L_{\max} \cdot B \text{ bits} = \frac{D \cdot L_{\max} \cdot B}{8} \text{ Bytes}$$

For $D = 5, L_{\max} = 32$:
- Float32 ($B=32$): $\mathcal{S}_{\min} = 5 \times 32 \times 4 = \mathbf{640 \text{ Bytes}}$ (+ metadata).
- Float16 ($B=16$): $\mathcal{S}_{\min} = 5 \times 32 \times 2 = \mathbf{320 \text{ Bytes}}$ (+ metadata).
- Int8 ($B=8$): $\mathcal{S}_{\min} = 5 \times 32 \times 1 = \mathbf{160 \text{ Bytes}}$ (+ metadata).

Any claim that an algorithm can preserve **exact, arbitrary point lookup** on IID streams using significantly fewer bytes than $\frac{D \cdot L_{\max} \cdot B}{8}$ violates Shannon's source coding theorem.

## 3. Four Distinct Compression Paradigms & Their Trade-Offs

| Compression Paradigm | Preserves Coordinates? | Preserves Amplitude? | Requires Redundancy? | Failure Mode on IID Stress |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Lossless Compression** (e.g. Huffman, LZ) | YES | YES | **YES ($I(X_t; X_{t-k}) > 0$)** | **Zero compression** on IID noise; memory expands due to tree overhead. |
| **2. Lossy Quantization** (FP16, INT8) | **YES** | APPROXIMATE ($q_t$) | **NO** (Works on any entropy) | Unbiased quantization noise increases estimator variance $\sigma_q^2$. |
| **3. Multirate Decimation** (Subsampling) | **NO** (Only decimated lags) | YES (at sampled points) | **YES (Bandlimited $\omega < \frac{\pi}{M}$)** | **Irreversible aliasing**; odd lags completely inaccessible; fails on IID. |
| **4. Polynomial Projection** (HiPPO / LMU) | APPROXIMATE | APPROXIMATE | **YES (Smooth Sobolev space)** | High polynomial degree needed for sharp impulses; ringing artifacts on noise. |

## 4. The Scientific Role of the Dual-Regime Design

To prevent misleading claims, this study establishes two contrasting evaluation regimes:
1. **Regime A (High-Entropy / IID Stress):**  
   Forces maximum-entropy inputs where lossy quantization (FP16, INT8) is the *only* viable compression method, directly testing the cost of exact temporal addressability.
2. **Regime B (Temporally Compressible AR / Bandlimited Streams):**  
   Provides genuine temporal mutual information ($I(x_t; x_{t-k}) \gg 0$), allowing multirate decimation and polynomial projections to function within their valid domain of mathematical assumptions.
