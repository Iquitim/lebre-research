- **Physical Root Causes:**
  1. *Table Transcription Bug:* In `scratch/generate_dynamic_lag_reports.py` (line 547), the markdown table generation string for B7 was duplicated identically into B7_E0.
  2. *Inactive Ablation Condition:* In `DynamicLagLifecycleModel.step()`, B7_E0 checked $|w| < 0.05$ without weight decay. Because unpenalized normalized LMS has zero gradient when $val = 0$, $w$ remained stationary near 0.76 throughout silence, preventing eviction in 37 of 40 runs.
- **Audited & Corrected Implementation (`B7_E0_CORRECTED_UNGATED`):**  
  Constructed under strict single-component isolation (Fostiropoulos & Itti, 2023): structural relevance updates continuously ($R_{t+1} = 0.999 R_t$), decaying toward zero during silence, and evicts when $R < \theta_{\text{evict}}$ **without** the two-timescale quiescence gate.
- **Reconciled Empirical Evidence (N=30 Fresh Confirmation Seeds `901`–`930`):**
  - B7 (Two-Timescale Quiescent Gate): **86.7% Unconditional Survival**, **100.0% Conditional Retention**, **0.0% False Eviction**, Post-100 NMSE = **0.4987**.
  - B7_E0 (Corrected Isolated Ablation): **0.0% Survival**, **76.7% False Eviction**, Median Eviction Time = **2,601 steps**, Post-100 NMSE = **1.0264** (+105.8% error surge upon signal return).
- **Final Verdict on H5:** **DECISIVELY SUPPORTED**.

### 2.2 Inconsistency B: Executive $p < 10^{-15}$ vs Section 3 $p = 1.73 \times 10^{-6}$
- **Combinatorial Boundary:** On $N=30$ paired differences without ties or zeros, the minimum possible two-sided signed-rank p-value is $2 / 2^{30} \approx \mathbf{1.8626 \times 10^{-9}}$. Any smaller value is mathematically impossible at $N=30$.
- **Source of $p = 1.73 \times 10^{-6}$:** Generated by `scipy.stats.wilcoxon(diff, method='approx')` (asymptotic Gaussian approximation with continuity correction) on the $N=30$ seed-level differences ($z = -4.772$).
- **Source of $p < 10^{-15}$:** Generated by **pseudoreplicated pooling** of all 90 task $\times$ seed observations ($3 \text{ tasks} \times 30 \text{ seeds}$) into `scipy.stats.wilcoxon` ($p = 1.74 \times 10^{-16}$).
- **Corrective Action:** The pooled claim $p < 10^{-15}$ is formally expunged. Certified seed-level inference is locked at exact $p = 1.86 \times 10^{-9}$ and asymptotic $p = 1.73 \times 10^{-6}$.
- **Source of Cohen's $d_z = 18.42$:** Generated from pooled task-by-seed data. The true seed-level Cohen's $d_z$ is **7.3799** (paired mean delta = 0.5404, std = 0.0732; still an immense effect).
- **Final Verdict on H1:** **SUPPORTED_UNCHANGED**.
