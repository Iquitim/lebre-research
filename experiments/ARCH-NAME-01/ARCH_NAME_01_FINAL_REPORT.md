## 1. Identity Summary

The architecture currently designated under the provisional working title **"Track B"** has achieved formal empirical validation (`BENCH-01B` sealed, 0.0% divergence across 450 runs, sub-100-FLOP operation) and rigorous contribution bounding (`CAR-01` completed). 

Its core scientific contribution is:
> **"A resource-governed online structural-learning architecture that treats observable features, temporal dependencies, and recurrent internal state as cost-bearing adaptive structures governed by a unified evidence-driven lifecycle under strict micro-resource budgets."**

Its persistent state machine coordinates structures across formal lifecycle phases:
$$\text{DORMANT} \longrightarrow \text{PROVISIONAL} \longrightarrow \text{ACTIVE} \longrightarrow \text{MATURE} \longrightarrow \text{EVICTED / RECLAIMED}$$

Key operational mechanisms:
- **Economic Parsimony:** Computational structures pay an operational maintenance rent ($c_j$) against accumulated utility ($U_j$).
- **Non-Interfering Shadow Probation:** Candidate structures learn in parallel shadow buffers without disturbing active output predictions.
- **Two-Timescale Quiescent Protection:** Observability Gramian proxies ($O_{\text{struct}}$) decouple structural necessity from instantaneous signal power ($O_{\text{obs}}$), preserving dormant memory across quiet intervals.
- **Asymmetric Eviction Hysteresis:** Positive statistical evidence of persistent disutility under 300:1 false-eviction loss weighting before deallocation.
- **Physical Resource Reclamation:** Deallocation contractually lowers per-step FLOP counters and persistent memory buffers.
- **Dynamic Compute Elasticity:** Scaling operational compute smoothly with task demand.

## 2. Naming Principles

To establish an enduring, respected scientific brand, the naming process adheres to five core principles:
1. **Pronounceability as a Natural Word:** 4–8 letters preferred (3–10 acceptable); phonetically fluid in English, Portuguese, and international scientific discourse.
2. **Representational Fidelity:** Evoking the architecture's core identity—resource governance, structural lifecycle, parsimony, and evidence-driven adaptation.
3. **Architectural Longevity:** Invariant to future scaling (e.g., $N > 1$ recurrent states, hierarchical structures, alternative optimizers, hardware substrates).
4. **Distinctiveness & Searchability:** Enabling clean citation and search engine discovery without collision against existing AI models, algorithms, or consumer brands.
5. **Absence of Hyperbole:** Free from embedded claims of optimality, universality, cognitive superiority, or unearned perfection.

## 3. Naming Constraints

The naming search strictly observed the following negative constraints:
- **No Implementation Artifacts:** No references to `Single-State`, `Scalar`, `N=1`, `RTRL`, `NLMS`, `GRU`, `RNN`.
- **No Embedded Claims:** No words such as `Optimal`, `Universal`, `General`, `Intelligent`, `Cognitive`, `SOTA`, `Supreme`.
- **No ASCA Association:** Complete independence from the prior ASCA project (no `ASCA-2`, `ASCA-NG`).
- **No Congested Buzzwords:** Exclusion of overused, generic technology names (e.g., `NOVA`, `CORE`, `NEXUS`, `FLOW`, `EDGE`).
- **No Artificial Acronyms:** Exclusion of forced, unpronounceable letter soups.
