## 1. Scientific Identity and Naming Charter

The architecture currently designated under the provisional working title **"Track B"** has achieved formal empirical validation (`BENCH-01B` sealed, 0.0% divergence across 450 runs, sub-100-FLOP operation) and rigorous contribution bounding (`CAR-01` completed). 

### 1.1 Core Architectural Identity
The essence of the architecture is defined as:
> **"A resource-governed online structural-learning architecture that treats observable features, temporal dependencies, and recurrent internal state as cost-bearing adaptive structures governed by a unified evidence-driven lifecycle under strict micro-resource budgets."**

Its persistent state machine coordinates structures across formal lifecycle phases:
$$\text{DORMANT} \longrightarrow \text{PROVISIONAL} \longrightarrow \text{ACTIVE} \longrightarrow \text{MATURE} \longrightarrow \text{EVICTED / RECLAIMED}$$

Key operational principles:
1. **Economic Parsimony:** Computational parameters are cost-bearing objects that must pay an operational "maintenance rent" ($c_j$) against accumulated utility ($U_j$).
2. **Non-Interfering Exploration:** Candidate structures are probed in a parallel shadow buffer without disturbing active output predictions.
3. **Two-Timescale Quiescent Protection:** Mathematical observability Gramian proxies ($O_{\text{struct}}$) decouple structural necessity from instantaneous signal power ($O_{\text{obs}}$), protecting dormant memory across quiet intervals.
4. **Asymmetric Eviction Hysteresis:** Structural destruction requires positive statistical evidence of persistent disutility under a severe 300:1 false-eviction loss weighting.
5. **Physical Resource Reclamation:** Deallocation contractually lowers per-step FLOP counters and persistent memory buffers.
6. **Dynamic Compute Elasticity:** Scaling operational compute smoothly with task demand.

### 1.2 What the Name Must NOT Be
The name must **not**:
- Imply current implementation limits (e.g., `Single-State`, `Scalar`, `N=1`, `RTRL`, `NLMS`, `GRU`, `RNN`).
- Embed unverified scientific claims (e.g., `Optimal`, `Universal`, `General`, `Intelligent`, `Cognitive`, `SOTA`, `Supreme`).
- Resemble or evoke ASCA (e.g., `ASCA-2`, `ASCA-NG`), as this architecture possesses an independent, distinct lineage.
- Rely on generic, congested buzzwords (e.g., `NOVA`, `CORE`, `NEXUS`, `FLOW`, `EDGE`).
- Be an artificial, unpronounceable alphabet soup.

## 3. Initial Filtering and Elimination Rationale

To narrow the 32 raw candidates down to an elite 10-name shortlist, a three-stage filter was applied:

### Filter 1: Semantic Fidelity Filter
- *Eliminated:* **SELVA** (implies wild unconstrained growth, contrary to sub-100-FLOP budget), **STASIS** (implies stagnation rather than plastic adaptation), **MOSA** (too generic, sounds like optimization algorithm).

### Filter 2: Pronounceability & Aesthetics Filter
- *Eliminated:* **RESL**, **RG-SL**, **ARCL**, **CSLA** (unpronounceable consonant clusters or letter strings), **FISCUS** (bureaucratic connotations), **CYCLUS** (awkward spelling/pronunciation).

### Filter 3: High Conflict & Trademark Risk Filter
- *Eliminated:*
  - **FORMA:** Severe commercial trademark conflict with *Autodesk Forma* (major generative AI architecture/CAD platform).
  - **OSLO:** Direct software conflict with *OSLO* (Hugging Face large-scale optimization framework).

  - **PRAXIS:** Highly congested across dozens of software and business tools.
  - **SOLA:** Highly congested across energy, music, and software products.
