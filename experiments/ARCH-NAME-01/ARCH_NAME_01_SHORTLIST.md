- **Trademark Risk:** **LOW**. *Nenhum conflito relevante identificado na busca preliminar.*

## 2. Multi-Criteria Rubric Scoring

Each candidate was scored from 1 (poor) to 5 (exceptional) across eight standardized criteria:
1. **SCIENTIFIC_FIT:** Accuracy in reflecting resource governance and the structural lifecycle.
2. **PRONUNCIABILITY:** Ease of spoken execution in English, Portuguese, and international settings.
3. **MEMORABILITY:** Stickiness, narrative appeal, and branding resonance.
4. **DISTINCTIVENESS:** Uniqueness in the scientific literature.
5. **SEARCHABILITY:** Likelihood of clean discovery via search engines.
6. **LONGEVITY:** Invariance to future architectural evolution ($N > 1$, new optimizers).
7. **LOW_COLLISION:** Freedom from conflicting models, frameworks, and trademarks.
8. **ACRONYM_NATURALNESS:** Elegance and plausibility of the expanded phrase.
